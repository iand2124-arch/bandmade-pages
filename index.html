Less organising.
More playing.

BandApp brings songs, set lists and band events together, with a performance view for rehearsals and gigs.

A personal project created and operated by Ian Downes.

Made for the band

  • Organise bands, song charts and performance keys.
  • Build event set lists and view them during performances.
  • Plan gigs and rehearsals with dates, times and venues.
  • Save Spotify track links alongside songs.

Google Calendar

With the connected account holder’s permission, BandApp creates, updates and deletes corresponding events in a selected Google Calendar when band events are saved or removed.

Event titles, band names, dates, times, venues, addresses and notes may be sent to Google to keep those events in sync. The integration requests the Google Calendar events permission.

See the privacy policy for details about permissions, storage and disconnecting Calendar.

Private by design

BandApp runs on a privately managed server and is intended for the owner and authorised users. This public website describes the app; it does not provide access to the app, its song library or its events.

BandApp is a personal project, not a public signup service. Google Calendar and Spotify are third-party services; BandApp is not affiliated with or endorsed by either provider.

Contact

For help with BandApp or a privacy question, contact Ian Downes through your existing contact channel. General project enquiries can also be raised through the public information-site repository. Do not post calendar details, credentials or other private information in public issues.
